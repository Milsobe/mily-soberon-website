THE BOOK

In Escape from Communism: 1962, Mily Soberon shares the true story of her childhood in Cuba during the Revolution and her family’s courageous journey to freedom. Through firsthand memories and reflections, she preserves a deeply personal chapter of Cuban history while reminding readers of the enduring importance of faith, family, freedom, and hope.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Mily Soberon

Mily Soberon is an American author and memoirist. In Escape from Communism: 1962, she shares the true story of her childhood in Cuba during the Communist Revolution and her family’s courageous journey to freedom.

Through her firsthand memories and reflections, she preserves a deeply personal chapter of Cuban history while reminding readers of the enduring importance of faith, family, freedom, and hope.

WHERE MY STORY BEGAN

Varadero, Cuba

Mily was born and raised in Varadero, just one block from the beautiful turquoise sea. Her grandfather worked tirelessly to build the family home and provide for his thirteen children. After the Revolution, the property was taken from the family and later converted into a restaurant.

“This home stands as a reminder of the life they loved, the freedom they lost, and why this story must be told.”
